關於我 About Me

您好！我是胡振惟，畢業於國立聯合大學資訊管理學系。 在學期間，我透過課程、專題與產學合作，累積 Android 應用程式開發、藍牙通訊、資料庫應用及影像辨識等實作經驗。

在產學合作專案中，我主要負責 Android 藍牙資料接收系統的開發， 工作內容包含介面設計、藍牙資料接收與解析、資料上傳及異常處理。 近一年的開發與測試過程中，我透過 Log 紀錄追蹤系統問題、 分析異常情況，並依據測試結果調整程式邏輯，累積系統測試、問題分析與除錯經驗。

此外，我也運用 Python、Flask 與 MySQL 開發 LINE Bot 圖書管理系統，並自行設計與製作個人作品集網站。 透過不同類型的專案，我接觸了行動應用、前後端開發及資料庫應用，也逐步熟悉從功能實作到測試與調整的開發過程。

未來，我希望將所累積的實作經驗應用於實際工作， 從資訊系統維護、功能測試、問題排查及資料處理等工作中持續學習，逐步提升技術能力與實務經驗。 歡迎透過本網站進一步了解我的專案成果與技術應用。

Hello! My name is Zhen-Wei Hu, and I graduated from the Department of Information Management at National United University. During my university studies, I gained hands-on experience in Android application development, Bluetooth communication, database applications, and image recognition through coursework, projects, and industry collaboration.

In an industry collaboration project, I was primarily responsible for developing an Android-based Bluetooth data reception system. My work included interface design, Bluetooth data reception and parsing, data uploads, and error handling. Through nearly a year of development and testing, I learned to use log records to investigate system issues, analyze abnormal behavior, and adjust program logic based on test results. This experience helped me develop practical skills in system testing, troubleshooting, and problem analysis.

In addition, I developed a LINE Bot book management system using Python, Flask, and MySQL, and independently designed and built my personal portfolio website. Through these projects, I gained experience in mobile applications, front-end and back-end development, and database applications. I also became more familiar with the process of implementing, testing, and refining software features.

I hope to apply my project experience in a professional environment and continue developing my skills through system maintenance, functional testing, troubleshooting, and data processing. I invite you to explore this website to learn more about my projects and technical experience.
